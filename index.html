Welcome

You will study 18 words across three short blocks. Each block uses a different strategy. Afterward, you will complete a brief counting task and then recall as many studied words as you can.

Important: Study each word carefully. Do not write words down or take screenshots. During recall, enter as many studied words as you remember, separated by commas or spaces.
